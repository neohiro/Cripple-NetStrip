{%- comment -%} Scroll affordances for the chip strip. Open Stage Island is the only site with in-page sections and it has fifteen of them, which is wider than a phone viewport or a laptop at 1.0 zoom, so the strip has to scroll -- and a strip that scrolls with no visible affordance just looks like a truncated nav. assets/js/bottom-bar.js reveals these only while the strip actually overflows, so on the three sites that have no chips they never appear at all, and it disables whichever one points past the end of the strip. {%- endcomment -%} {%- comment -%} No tabindex on the scroll buttons. They were shipped with tabindex="-1" to keep them out of the tab order, but that only hides them when they are hidden: the buttons are `display: none` until the strip actually overflows, and `display: none` already removes an element from the tab order. So the attribute did nothing while hidden and, once the strip overflowed and the buttons became visible and enabled, it kept a visible, operable control unreachable by keyboard -- with :focus-visible styling that could never fire. Verified: prev.tabIndex was -1, prev.focusableByKeyboard false, and prev.focus() did not move focus. `disabled` still removes a button from the tab order on its own, which is what handles the at-each-end state. {%- endcomment -%}
